Structured Giving & Advocacy

Five Responsible Ways You Can Make an Impact

CareBridge does not accept donations directly. Instead, we empower you to support verified institutions through their official channels.

Support Established Organisations

Direct financial support to verified hospital welfare funds. Zakat and Sadaqah directly subsidize chemotherapy, advanced radiation therapy, and complex surgical procedures for impoverished patients.

Verified Channels: Shaukat Khanum, Bait-ul-Sukoon, Pink Ribbon, Indus Hospital.
View Official Hospital Portals → Secure external official links only

Volunteer Your Time

Explore structured volunteering programmes with established oncology centers. Opportunities include pediatric ward reading, bedside companionship, blood donor drives, and organizing patient library carts.

Typical Commitment: 2-4 hours weekly · Hospital orientation required.
Inquire About Volunteering → Vetted by institutional guidelines

Share Reliable Information

Combat harmful cancer misinformation and unverified herbal cures that delay critical diagnosis. Share verified checklists, hospital contact numbers, and government welfare procedures with your community.

Impact: Prevents treatment abandonment and dangerous diagnostic delays.
Explore Resource Directory → Shareable verified data

Raise Responsible Awareness

Organize respectful awareness talks in schools, universities, and workplaces. Educate families about early symptoms (breast lumps, persistent coughs, unexplained bruising in children) and screening clinics.

Key Initiatives: Pinktober Breast Awareness · Childhood Cancer Month.
Read Awareness Insights → Dignified educational materials

Connect With Support Organisations

Are you an established NGO, cancer hospice, or patient welfare trust? Submit your published welfare guidelines, contact details, and assistance criteria for inclusion in the CareBridge directory after review.

Review Policy: Subject to legal registration and verified public criteria.
Submit Organisation Information → Editorial vetting before publishing

Our Donation Transparency & Anti-Intermediary Commitment

CareBridge does not collect donations, operate payment merchant accounts, or touch philanthropic funds. Cancer treatment funding is sensitive and sacred. To ensure 100% of your contribution reaches deserving patients, we only direct you to the official, audited payment channels of verified healthcare institutions. Always confirm bank account titles match the official hospital legal name.

Direct Official Donation Channels:

Shaukat Khanum Zakat & Welfare Portal Bait-ul-Sukoon Free Cancer Care Portal Pink Ribbon Pakistan Breast Hospital Fund Indus Hospital & Health Network Free Care
