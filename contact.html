Mission, Ethics & Inquiries

About CareBridge & Contact Desk

CareBridge was built on a core human conviction: that finding cancer support should never be another barrier. Discover our social mission, safety boundaries, and how to reach our project team.

Connecting People. Sharing Hope. Supporting Lives.

When an individual is diagnosed with cancer in Pakistan, the immediate psychological shock is frequently compounded by practical anxiety. Information regarding charitable welfare funds, specialized pediatric centers, and government hospital quotas is scattered across different websites and hospital information sources. For a family travelling from distant areas, a missing form can result in weeks of delay.

CareBridge was developed as a student educational platform to bring clarity, dignity, and technology-based guidance to this landscape. By bringing institutional guidelines together in an accessible directory and providing a Support Assistant, CareBridge helps families understand where to go, what documents to prepare, and who to contact.

Our Core Commitments

  • Unbiased Consolidation: We independently index official hospital welfare criteria without commercial favoritism.
  • Clear Accessibility: Easy-to-understand information helps families understand requirements and next steps.
  • Dignity & Privacy First: We do not collect or store confidential patient medical records or identity cards.
Crucial Ethics & Safety Boundaries

Medical & Guidance Boundaries

The CareBridge Support Assistant is an educational navigation system, not a doctor. We follow these safety rules:

  • Zero Clinical Diagnosis: The assistant will never diagnose cancer or suggest treatment changes.
  • No Replacement for Oncologists: Medical questions must be directed to licensed healthcare professionals.
  • No Financial Guarantees: CareBridge cannot guarantee approval of financial grants.
  • Verification Required: Users must confirm hospital policies with official welfare departments.
