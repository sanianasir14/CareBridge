Support Resources & Awareness Hub

Navigating cancer assistance requires accurate facts, organized preparation, and transparent instructions. Explore our verified directory, preparation checklists, and educational awareness media.

When cancer touches a family, medical jargon and bureaucratic paperwork can feel like an insurmountable maze. Every patient deserves transparent access to help.

In Pakistan, established institutions such as Shaukat Khanum Memorial Cancer Hospital, Bait-ul-Sukoon, and the federal Pakistan Bait-ul-Mal programme have disbursed billions of rupees in treatment subsidies. However, many families fail to secure assistance simply because they lack the specific preliminary biopsy documents, or approach the wrong department without an official Medical Superintendent endorsement.

CareBridge was conceived to address this information gap. By organizing verified criteria into easily searchable formats and providing step-by-step checklists, we help families arrive at the hospital prepared, confident, and supported.

► How to Explore Support Resources (Step-by-Step)

Follow this sequential roadmap when seeking financial or clinical support:

  1. Obtain Confirmed Pathology & Imaging: Assistance programmes require written histopathology (biopsy) confirming diagnosis before opening a welfare file.
  2. Identify Regional Facilities: Filter our Resource Table below by your city (e.g. Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Rawalpindi) to find empaneled institutions.
  3. Consult the CareBridge Support Assistant: Use the bottom-right assistant to clarify complex terms, check required forms, and navigate welfare instructions.
  4. Schedule Walk-In Triage: Visit the hospital's patient welfare department in morning hours with all required documents.

✓ Document Checklist Before Contacting Organisations

Prepare copies of these essential items to prevent delays during hospital evaluation:

  • Original & 3 photocopies of Patient CNIC or NADRA B-Form (for minors)
  • Recent Histopathology Biopsy Report & CT/PET/MRI Imaging Films
  • Treating Oncologist's Prescription & Estimated Cost Breakdown
  • Household Income Verification: Salary slip, pension book, or local Union Council income affidavit
  • Paid Copy of Recent Electricity & Gas Utility Bills (Proof of Residence)
  • Two passport-size photographs of patient and nominated attendant
