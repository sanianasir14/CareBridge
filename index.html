How CareBridge Guides You

Clear, compassionate steps to move from uncertainty to informed action.

01

Explore Support Resources

Browse structured directories of verified hospitals, government assistance schemes, and early screening centers with transparent criteria.

02

Consult the Support Assistant

Ask questions anytime via our FAQ Support Assistant. It clarifies application guidelines, required document checklists, and local support options.

03

Contact Official Channels Directly

Trust & Information Integrity Notice

CareBridge is an educational and navigational platform designed to consolidate publicly available cancer support information. We do not provide clinical diagnosis, determine personal eligibility, or issue financial grants. Always verify current application requirements directly with the respective hospital or charitable organization.
